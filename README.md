# The Royal Dine & Cafe - Fine Dining & Restaurant Landing Page

A luxury, high-converting digital presence and direct ordering website designed for fine dining restaurants and cafes.

## Key Highlights & Features
- **0% Commission Direct WhatsApp Orders:** Customers order their favorite dishes with direct instant kitchen dispatch.
- **Table Reservation System:** Instant weekend/party table booking form directly routed to WhatsApp.
- **Chef's Signature Digital Menu:** Dynamic item cards with dietary tags (Veg, Chef Special, Bestsellers) and pricing.
- **Mobile-First Luxury Aesthetic:** Gold & Charcoal ambient dark mode inspired by 5-star hospitality standards.
- **Google Maps & Review Badges:** 4.8★ social proof and instant customer trust elements.
